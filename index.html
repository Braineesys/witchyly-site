Core Gameplay

Catch. Match. Brew.

Slip under the rain of falling ingredients, move your cauldron and catch the right elements to brew magical concoctions… without letting it all overflow!

01 — Catch

Catch Ingredients

Ingredients rain down from above. Move Ly's cauldron swiftly across the floor and catch the right ingredients before they fall.

02 — Match

Match Families

Combine the right ingredient families. Good combinations create powerful magical brews; bad ones descend into chaos!

03 — Brew

Brew Potions

Create potions, progress through levels, unlock rare recipes and fill Ly's enchanted grimoire to master the craft.
