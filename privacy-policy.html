Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 — Vespera (WitchyLy)
Notice to Players & Visitors: This Privacy Policy governs your use of the website witchyly.com and the mobile application WitchyLy, developed by Vespera. Certain technical implementations (such as specific telemetry frameworks or ad networks) must be verified and configured by Vespera prior to commercial distribution.

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to WitchyLy. Your privacy is paramount to us at Vespera. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit our website or download and play WitchyLy on iOS (via Apple TestFlight) and Android (via Google Play Store).

2. Distinguishing Website Data vs. Mobile Game Data

A. Website (witchyly.com)

When you browse our landing website, we collect minimal technical connection data, such as your IP address, browser type, referring URL, and language preference. We use browser local storage (localStorage) solely to remember your chosen language (English, French, or Arabic). No third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels are deployed on the static landing page.

B. Mobile Game (WitchyLy App)

The WitchyLy game application runs locally on your mobile device. Core game progress (completed rooms, unlocked grimoire recipes, level milestones, coin balances, and scores) is stored locally on your device storage unless cloud synchronization is enabled.

3. Third-Party Distribution Platforms

WitchyLy is distributed via authorized third-party app distribution services:

  • Apple TestFlight: If you participate in our iOS beta testing via TestFlight, Apple collects diagnostic and crash logs pursuant to the Apple Beta Software Program terms and Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store: Android downloads and platform services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

Vespera does not collect or store your payment card details, billing addresses, or personal store account passwords.

4. Analytics, Telemetry & Crash Reporting

To ensure WitchyLy remains bug-free and optimized across thousands of device models, anonymous telemetry data (such as frame rate metrics, device model, operating system version, and crash logs) may be processed in an aggregated, non-identifiable format. [Note: Vespera must verify the specific telemetry SDKs embedded in production builds.]

5. Leaderboards & Social Features

WitchyLy features an apprentice leaderboard allowing players to benchmark their high scores and Grimoire completion rates. If an online leaderboard is active, scores may be transmitted alongside a chosen player pseudonym or anonymous identifier. No real-world contact information is publicly published on leaderboards.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & International Standards)

WitchyLy is a wholesome, casual fantasy mobile game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable legal age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact Vespera so we may promptly purge such records.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes local storage to persist your selected interface language across sessions. You can clear local storage at any time via your browser settings.

8. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

We implement reasonable physical, technical, and administrative security measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. Players may reset their local game progress at any time by clearing game data via their device settings or uninstalling the application.

9. International Data Transfers

As a global game, data processed in connection with app store services may be stored or processed on servers situated outside your country of residence, subject to international data transfer safeguards.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Vespera reserves the right to update this policy periodically to reflect evolving legal requirements or game updates. Revisions will be posted here with an updated effective date.
